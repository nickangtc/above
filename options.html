Your site limits

Keep the time you spend online intentional. Each limit resets at midnight in your computer's timezone.

Include a domain (e.g. linkedin.com) to track it and all its subdomains. Only the active tab in the focused window counts, and timing pauses after 60 seconds without interaction. A five-minute active-use grace period is available once per site per day after the limit is reached. Removing a site does not erase today's recorded time if you add it back.
